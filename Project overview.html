A Study on the Impact of Paid Social Media Advertising on Small-Scale Businesses in Malappuram District

Investigator: Muhsira Pananchery • M.Com Scholar
analytics

Project Overview

This study examines the impact of paid social media advertising on small-scale businesses in Malappuram District, Kerala. It focuses on understanding how paid advertising through social media platforms relates to customer trust, physical store visits, and business sales.

The study aims to explore the role of paid social media advertising in connecting online promotional activities with customer responses and business performance.

Methodology psychology_alt

Descriptive & Analytical Framework

The study follows a descriptive and analytical research approach, bringing together the perspectives of small-scale business owners, consumers, and social media users.
